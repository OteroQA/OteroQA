## 🧭 Who I am

> Software testing is not a phase at the end of the sprint. It is the reason your release does not turn into an incident.

I am **Damián Otero**, founder and CEO of **[OteroQA](https://oteroqa.com)**, a QA and software testing agency based in Ourense, Galicia, working remotely with clients across Europe.

We work as an **external QA team plugged into your process**, not as a black box that shows up at the end. We understand the product, prioritise by risk and impact, and report findings in plain language, in Spanish or English.
